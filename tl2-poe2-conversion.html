Twelve Kits of Vilderan

Torchlight II skill trees rebuilt as Path of Exile 2 weapon kits
CONVERSION · 12 TREES · 84 SKILLS
The conversion in one line. Torchlight II gates each tree's skills at character levels 1 / 7 / 14 / 21 / 28 / 35 / 42 — seven rungs. Path of Exile 2 gates weapon skills at Uncut Gem tiers 1 / 3 / 5 / 7 / 9 / 11 / 13 — also seven. That let every tree map rung-for-rung onto a weapon ladder with nothing invented to make it fit. Each of the twelve trees then gets what a PoE 2 weapon kit has: a free opener, a mobility skill, a private state nothing else touches, at least one closed loop, and a deliberate hole its sibling trees fill.

What is original and what is mine. Skill names marked TL2 are real Torchlight II skills, most with their original unlock level; those marked NEW are designs I wrote to fill a ladder rung where I could not verify a TL2 skill for that slot. Every mechanical description on this page is new. Nothing here describes how a skill actually behaves in Torchlight II — that is the entire point of a conversion, but it means this is not a TL2 reference and should not be used as one. For the real game, see the Torchlight Wiki.

Torchlight II is Runic Games' work and Path of Exile 2 is Grinding Gear Games'. This is unaffiliated fan design built on the interaction model from the Wraeclast Weapon Codex.
