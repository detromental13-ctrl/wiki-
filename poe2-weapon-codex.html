Wraeclast Weapon Codex

Weapon skill trees & their interactions
PATCH 0.5.x · RETURN OF THE ANCIENTS
How to read this codex. Path of Exile 2 has no literal per-weapon passive tree. What it has is two systems that together behave like one: skills are gated behind Uncut Skill Gem tiers (Lv. 1 / 3 / 5 / 7 / 9 / 11 / 13) and filtered by the weapon in your hand, and the passive tree carries 24 Weapon Set passive points that let you grow a second, separate specialisation for your off-set weapon. Each page below is one weapon's gem ladder; the Interaction Atlas is where the two sets meet.

Accuracy note. Skill rosters and gem tiers here reflect the 0.5.x Return of the Ancients era and are assembled from community databases and build guides, which disagree with each other on exact numbers more often than you would hope. Entries marked † are ones I could not corroborate — treat the tier as a best guess and confirm at a Gemcutting bench. Mechanics and interactions are the durable part; individual tier numbers are the part patch notes move. Swords and the Duelist are not in 0.5.x — they arrive at 1.0.
